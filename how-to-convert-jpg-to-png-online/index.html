JPG vs PNG: what changes?

JPEG commonly uses lossy compression and is widely used for photographs. PNG uses lossless compression and supports transparency. These characteristics do not mean that converting a JPEG into PNG recreates information discarded when the JPEG was made.

  • Photographs: JPEG is often practical when smaller file sizes matter.
  • Graphics and screenshots: PNG can be useful when crisp edges or lossless storage are important.
  • Transparency: PNG can store transparent pixels, but a JPEG source does not contain transparency information to recover.

Does converting JPG to PNG improve image quality?

No. Conversion changes the output format, not the detail available in the source. If a JPEG contains compression artefacts, converting it to PNG preserves those visible artefacts. Avoid repeated conversions and keep the best-quality original available.

Practical tip: If you need a sharper image, start with a higher-quality original rather than expecting format conversion to repair blur or compression damage.

Will the converted PNG have a transparent background?

No, not automatically. JPEG does not support transparency. A JPG-to-PNG conversion carries over the visible image, including its existing background. To make a background transparent, you need a separate editing or background-removal workflow and should inspect the result carefully.

Common issues and solutions

The file picker does not show my image

Check that you selected the correct folder and that the file is actually a JPG or JPEG. Try opening the image in your device's gallery first.

The PNG file is larger than the JPG

This can happen because PNG and JPEG use different compression methods. A PNG copy of a photograph may take more storage space. If a small file is your priority, consider the image compressor and inspect the final visual quality.

The image looks no better after conversion

That is expected. Conversion does not restore details lost in the original JPEG. Use a higher-quality source if available.

The background is still visible

JPG-to-PNG conversion does not remove backgrounds. Use a separate editing workflow if transparency is required.

Before you use or share the converted file

  • Keep the original JPG until the PNG meets your needs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert JPG to PNG online for free?

Yes. PixelToolkit provides a browser-based JPG to PNG converter without requiring a desktop installation.

Is JPG the same as JPEG?

JPG and JPEG refer to the same image format. The shorter .jpg extension is commonly used because of historical filename conventions.

Does JPG to PNG conversion improve quality?

No. It changes the file format but cannot recover detail lost during JPEG compression.

Does converting JPG to PNG make the background transparent?

No. JPEG does not store transparency. The converted PNG retains the visible background from the JPG.

Why is my PNG larger than the original JPG?

PNG and JPEG compress image data differently. PNG files of photographs can be larger than their JPEG sources.
