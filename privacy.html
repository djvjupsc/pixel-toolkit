Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice describes the current intended operation of PixelToolkit. It must be updated if the site adds analytics, advertising, accounts, server-side processing or other data collection.

Images and files

Core image-processing features are designed to process selected files locally in your browser. The static website does not intentionally upload those image files to a PixelToolkit application server. Some tools may load third-party libraries over the internet. Browser, device, extensions and network providers may process data under their own policies. Do not use a shared device for confidential files.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted through Cloudflare Pages and its source is maintained in a GitHub repository. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP address, request details, security events and logs under their own policies. PixelToolkit does not currently describe a separate account system or user database.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may be configured to display advertising in the future, including through Google AdSense. No advertising or analytics service should be considered active unless its code has actually been installed. If advertising or analytics is added, this policy must be updated to identify the services, cookies or similar technologies used, relevant purposes, user controls and applicable consent choices. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve or measure ads as described in their own policies.

External links

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices.

Children and sensitive information

Do not submit sensitive personal information through public feedback channels. The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not designed to collect personal profiles.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when site functionality changes. The date at the top should be revised when material changes are made.

Contact
