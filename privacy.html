Privacy Notice

This starter app performs supported image operations locally in your browser. It does not intentionally upload selected image files to a server.

Browser capabilities, third-party libraries, hosting logs and any future analytics or API integrations may affect data handling. Before public launch, review all dependencies and update this notice to describe actual behavior accurately.

Do not upload highly sensitive images unless you understand the risks of the device and browser you are using.
