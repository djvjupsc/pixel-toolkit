Does JPG to PNG restore lost quality?

No. Converting a JPEG to PNG does not recover detail lost during earlier JPEG compression. PNG stores the converted pixels in a lossless format, but the source quality remains the limiting factor.

Transparency note

JPEG does not support transparency. Converting a JPG to PNG does not create a transparent background; the resulting image retains the visible pixels from the source.

Frequently asked questions

Is JPG to PNG conversion free?

Yes. The conversion tool is available in PixelToolkit without an account.

Will the converted PNG look sharper?

No. Changing the container format cannot restore detail that is absent from the JPEG source.

Does the PNG have a transparent background?

No. A JPG has no transparency information to preserve.

Are images uploaded?
