How to use this tool

  1. Select an image: Choose a supported image from your device.
  2. Open the PDF tool: The Image to PDF workspace will be selected automatically.
  3. Create the PDF: PixelToolkit places the image on a PDF page and creates the document locally in your browser.
  4. Download and review: Open the PDF after downloading and check page orientation, margins and image clarity.

Page layout and image sizing

The current converter places the image on a standard portrait A4-sized PDF page and scales it to fit. It does not offer advanced page-size, margin or orientation controls.

Multiple images

The separate Multiple Images to PDF tool can create a multi-page document from selected images. Review the resulting PDF before submitting or sharing it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert JPG to PDF?

Yes. Select a supported image and use the Image to PDF tool.

Does it upload my image?

The standard conversion creates the PDF in your browser on your device.

Can I combine several images?

Use PixelToolkit's separate Multiple Images to PDF tool for a multi-page PDF.

Can I choose A4 or landscape layout?

The current single-image converter uses a standard portrait A4 page and does not expose advanced page-layout controls.
